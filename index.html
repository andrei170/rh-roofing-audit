Your Whole Website Is 367 Words, And Your Phone Number Is Not Tappable On Any Of Them.

367
words on your entire site

Most roofing enquiries are a phone call made from a mobile, standing in a garden, looking up.

Your number is printed on the page as text. It is not a link, so tapping it does nothing. The customer has to memorise it, close your site, open the keypad and type it in.

A good proportion of them will not.

Site and sitemap read 24 September 2026
Finding 1 — The Site Is A Business Card
Homepage word count 367 Pages in your sitemap 1 Sections on the homepage: Welcome · Our services · Our projects · Feedback Tappable phone links 0 Enquiry forms 0 Structured data none
There is nothing wrong with it. There is just very little of it.

Three hundred and sixty-seven words is about one side of A4. It covers everything: who you are, what you do, projects and feedback.

Google cannot rank a page for something it does not discuss. Somebody in Devon searching for flat roof repair, slate work, a leak, or a roofer in their particular town will not find you, because none of those has a page.

And there is no form anywhere, so the only way to reach you is to type a number in manually.

Finding 2 — Nothing Is Measured At All
Google Analytics not found Google Tag Manager not found Google Ads tag not found Meta pixel not found Call tracking not found
You cannot tell whether the website has ever produced a single job

There is no tracking of any kind. Nobody knows how many people visit, what they look at, or whether any of them ever ring.

That matters more than it sounds. Right now the site is either quietly producing work or doing nothing at all, and there is no way to know which. Analytics is free and takes minutes.

Finding 3 — Two Numbers, Neither Of Them Linked
Published on the site: 01392 841167 a landline 07703 989767 a mobile Tappable: neither
Two separate issues

First, the tap. On a phone, a number written as plain text does nothing when touched. Making both numbers proper links is a five-minute change and it is the highest-return thing on this page.

Second, which number is the business? Two numbers split your listings and your reviews over time, and neither is tracked. Google Business Profile wants one primary number.

What We Would Do, In Order
  • Make both numbers tappable. Five minutes, and it is the difference between a call and a closed tab.
  • Put analytics on, so within a month you know whether the site does anything.
  • Add an enquiry form, for the people who will not ring — which on roofing is often the bigger, planned jobs rather than the leaks.
  • Write a page per service — flat roofing, slate, tile, repairs, and whatever else you want more of.
  • Then a page per town you actually want to drive to.
  • Claim and build the Google Business Profile, and start asking for reviews. On local roofing that is where most enquiries begin, and it is free.

One page, no form, and a phone number nobody can tap.

Everything on this list is small. That is what makes it worth doing.
