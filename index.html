Three Findings
One — no tap-to-call, and no form on the page people land on
randhroofing.co.uk · served source · 24 Aug 2026 homepage tel: links 0 <form> tags 0 inputs 0 feedback tel: links 0 <form> tags 0 inputs 0 contact tel: links 0 <form> tags 1 inputs 8

The contact page has a real eight-field form, which is fine. The homepage has no form and no clickable number at all. The only route in from the front page is a mailto: link.

Adding tel: links is a ten-minute job on WordPress and it is the single highest-return change on this list.

Two — your newest customer review is eight years old
Feedback page — 12 testimonials, all dated: 2017 × 6 2018 × 6 newest: 15th August 2018 2019 – 2026: nothing

They are genuine, named and specific — lead valleys, chimney repair, a loft condensation problem, new downpipes and guttering. That is exactly the right kind of proof.

But a visitor reads a wall of 2017 and 2018 dates and concludes the business peaked eight years ago. The page even carries the heading "Please leave us feedback" — with no form under it to leave any. Nobody can, so nobody has.

Three — nothing on the site is measured, and nothing is ad-ready
Google Analytics / GA4 ✗ none Google Tag Manager ✗ none Google Ads tag ✗ none Meta pixel ✗ none Structured data / schema ✗ none

If you spent money on advertising tomorrow, not one click could be traced to a job. You would be trusting whoever ran it, because there would be no independent way to check. That is how most roofers end up believing advertising does not work — they cannot see whether it did.

No Meta pixel also means no retargeting audience exists. Every person who has visited your site and not called is gone for good. Installing the pixel costs nothing and starts building that list from today, whether or not you ever advertise.

Priority Roadmap — Fix First (All Free)
  • Make both numbers tap-to-call on every page. Ten minutes, and it is the biggest single thing costing you calls
  • Put the ten-year guarantee at the top of the homepage. It is your strongest sentence and it is buried
  • Ask your last twenty customers for a Google review. Eight years of jobs, nobody asked
  • Put a form on the homepage, not only on the contact page
Build Next — Compounding Growth
  • Install analytics and the Meta pixel now — before any spend, so a retargeting audience starts building from today
  • Rebuild the form to qualify: roof type, job type, property, postcode, urgency
  • Add LocalBusiness / RoofingContractor schema so Google knows what you are and where
  • Town pages for Exeter, Exmouth, Newton Abbot, Tiverton, Honiton and Crediton instead of "anywhere in Devon"

Twenty-five years, a ten-year guarantee, and a phone number nobody can tap.
